How I run AI.

Most people use AI to guess faster. I teach it to work from facts, the same way a good ops lead would.

Facts, not guesses

If the AI can't point to where a fact came from, it gets labeled a guess or cut.

Drafts, not sends

AI writes the follow-up. You read it and hit send. Nothing goes to a customer without a human.

Memory in files

Your pipeline, notes and handoffs live in files you own, so nothing depends on one chat window.
